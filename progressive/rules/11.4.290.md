### §11.4.290 — Optimistic done with parallel background verification: PROVISIONAL-DONE vs VERIFIED-DONE, automatic non-silent reopen, honest release labelling (User mandate, 2026-10-27)

**Forensic anchor — verbatim operator mandate (2026-10-27 15:13 +05):** "Optimistic, marking things done, releasing ASAP versions, but validating and verifying against machine evidence ALWAYS in parallel in background! If we encounter issues we shall reopen! Until it is proved not done, consider it done! This shall be / must be / it is rule we MUST follow! Make sure this is clear from our constitution (Submodule) as well!"

**The tension this anchor resolves (stated so the two mandates are never read as contradicting).** Two operator mandates bind the same words. §11.4.123 (2026-06-03): "Nothing can be considered fixed or completed without hard evidence!" — and §7.1 / §11.4.1 / §11.4.6 forbid any PASS claim without captured evidence. This mandate (2026-10-27): planning, tracking and releasing proceed optimistically while verification runs in parallel. Both hold at once because they govern different WORDS and different DECISIONS. The words "fixed", "implemented", "completed", "verified", "validated", "passing" and "green" stay evidence-gated exactly as before. The DECISIONS to plan on an item as done, unblock its dependents, count it in the roadmap and ship it are no longer gated on the verification verdict; they are gated on a weaker, separately named and separately labelled state, PROVISIONAL-DONE, which claims only "implemented and wired, verification scheduled" and never "works".

**(A) THE RULE.** When the implementing agent judges an item implemented AND wired (§11.4.197: used by default, not parked behind a dead flag), the item is marked done at once, without waiting for the verification chain, and work proceeds: dependents unblock, the roadmap and burndown count it, a release may be cut ASAP. Verification is not skipped, deferred or optional: it runs ALWAYS, in PARALLEL with continuing work, in the BACKGROUND, against MACHINE evidence (§11.4.262 / §11.4.207: a harness-written verdict, artifact path + sha256 + timestamp). "Until it is proved not done, consider it done": the presumption of done is the planning default and is rebutted only by contrary evidence (D). It is never promoted to "verified" by the passage of time or by the absence of a failure (silence is not verification, §11.4.6 / §7.1).

**(B) STATE MODEL — closed set, three names.**

| State | Meaning | Carrier (existing Status vocabulary, unchanged) | Counts as done for planning, roadmap, release scope | May be called verified / fixed / passing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `PROVISIONAL-DONE` (alias `done-provisional`) | Implemented + wired; a background verification job is scheduled; no verdict yet | Status `Ready for testing` or `In testing` (§11.4.15) plus a `**Verification:** provisional` line | YES | NEVER |
| `VERIFIED-DONE` | The §11.4.108 runtime signature verified on a clean target, evidence class at or above the §11.4.226 floor, captured proof per §11.4.123 | The §11.4.33 type-aware closure value (`Fixed` / `Implemented` / `Completed (→ Fixed.md)`) | YES | YES |
| `REOPENED` | Contrary evidence exists (D) | Status `Reopened` plus `**Reopened-Details:**` (§11.4.34) | NO | NO |

The Status closed set of §11.4.15 / §11.4.21 / §11.4.33 / §11.4.90 is NOT extended: no generator, parser, schema or audit that keys on those literals changes meaning, and no tracker may overload `Fixed` / `Implemented` / `Completed` to mean provisional. The state is carried by a required `**Verification:**` line within eight non-blank lines of the item heading (the carrier pattern of §11.4.34 `Reopened-Details` and §11.4.21 `Operator-Block-Details`): `**Verification:** provisional — job <id> — checks <runtime signature> — layer <user-visible|runtime|artifact|source> — target <fingerprint> — artifact <fix fingerprint> — due <ISO timestamp>`, or `**Verification:** verified — evidence <path> sha256 <hash>`. The Fixed file is the §11.4.19 home of closed items only, so a PROVISIONAL-DONE item stays in the Issues file until it is verified and no PROVISIONAL-DONE item is ever a row of the Fixed file.

**(C) THE JOB EXISTS AT THE MOMENT OF MARKING.** An item may be marked PROVISIONAL-DONE only if its background verification job is ALREADY scheduled (scheduled-work queue, background agent or CI job; the §11.4.230 build-validate overlap) and recorded in the `Verification:` line, in the same commit or regeneration as the status write (§11.4.15). The job names: the single machine-checkable runtime signature (§11.4.108(2)); the evidence-class floor of its defect layer (§11.4.226(1)); the target and the fix's artifact fingerprint the check must read FROM the target (§11.4.115(F)); and a due time (the staleness budget, §11.4.226(6)). A marking without a scheduled job is refused and the item stays `In progress`. This pair is the §11.4.226(4) per-status evidence precondition of the state: source-class evidence of implemented + wired (source ref, the pre-build and commit gates green) AND the job record. A mitigation or a state-only repair (§11.4.226(5)) is not an implementation and is not PROVISIONAL-DONE either.

**(D) CONTRARY EVIDENCE AND THE AUTOMATIC, NON-SILENT REOPEN.** Contrary evidence is a verification check, of at least the declared layer's evidence class, that FAILs on an artifact whose fingerprint equals the fix's artifact fingerprint, on the same target, from an instrument validated by its control needle (§11.4.7 same-conditions rule; §11.4.201). When it exists the item reopens AUTOMATICALLY, by the job's own epilogue or by the next sweep within one development cycle, never by waiting for a person to notice: Status `Reopened`; `**Reopened-Details:**` per §11.4.34 (By: AI, On: ISO date, Reason from the closed vocabulary, normally `test-failed` or `captured-evidence-contradicts`, Evidence: the job's evidence path); the §11.4.55 reopen counter increments, and with it the §11.4.189 extra-scrutiny priority. The reopen is NEVER silent: the next generated changelog, roadmap, burndown, Issues and summary documents MUST show it by item id, reason and evidence path, and any release that shipped the item while provisional is marked `released provisional — now reopened`. NOT contrary evidence, and never grounds to reopen or to verify: a RED on a pre-fix artifact (the §11.4.115 RED baseline is the EXPECTED state of the unfixed artifact); a verdict on a different target or with a stale or mismatched fingerprint; a BLIND, ERROR or cannot-determine instrument outcome (§11.4.201: instrument failures are repaired, never counted as FAIL or as PASS); a verdict of a lower evidence class than the layer floor. The same filters bind promotion: a PASS on a pre-fix artifact, or below the floor, never promotes.

**(E) PROMOTION AND BOUNDEDNESS.** A PASS verdict at or above the floor class, on the matching artifact and target, with its machine fields (§11.4.226(2)) and the complete §11.4.108 / §11.4.123 chain, promotes the item to VERIFIED-DONE through the existing §11.4.15 / §11.4.33 closure and §11.4.19 migration. A job that is overdue, never executed, or BLIND past its due time neither demotes nor promotes: the item stays PROVISIONAL-DONE, is labelled `verification OVERDUE`, is surfaced in every roadmap and release note, and enters the standing risk-ordered re-run queue first (§11.4.226(6), §11.4.189). PROVISIONAL-DONE is a bounded interval and not a resting place: §11.4.197 (no third, un-wired state) and §11.4.226(4) (a status that sounds like progress becomes the parking lot of unverified work) apply, and the overdue count is a reported number, never a hidden one.

**(F) LABELLING.** Every artifact that reports item state (Issues, summaries, roadmap, burndown, changelog, release notes, status messages, agent replies, dashboards) uses exactly the labels `PROVISIONAL-DONE` and `VERIFIED-DONE` and reports the two counts SEPARATELY. A bare "N done" is non-compliant wherever either population is non-zero; it is written "V verified + P provisional (O overdue)". A PROVISIONAL-DONE item never appears in a verified, fixed-and-verified, passing or green count, and a sentence that calls it fixed, working or validated is a §11.4.6 / §7.1 bluff however the rest of the document is labelled.

**(G) RELEASE AND CHANGELOG DUTY.** A release (tag, image, build, QA-deploy hand-off) MAY ship ASAP with PROVISIONAL-DONE work in scope, within the release classes the consuming project declares provisional-eligible as DATA (§11.4.35); absent a declaration the eligible classes are the dev and pre-release classes only, and a class not declared eligible requires every in-scope item VERIFIED-DONE or removed from scope. The changelog / release notes MUST state, in this order: (1) VERIFIED — each item with its evidence path and sha256; (2) PROVISIONAL — each item with its pending job id, what the job checks, on which target and layer, its due time, and the exact command or queue entry by which the verification runs; (3) REOPENED since the previous release — each with reason and evidence path; (4) OVERDUE — each provisional item past its due time. Listing provisional work as verified is a §11.4.6 violation and a release blocker. This clause moves WHEN verification is required relative to shipping, for the named items only. It relaxes no seam that already refuses on the absence of a verdict for its registered guard set (§11.4.115(F), §11.4.135, §11.4.236): those keep refusing, and a specific seam is relaxed only through the explicit paths that already exist (§11.4.236(4) operator override, §11.4.271 waiver), never by this anchor. A gate that FAILs on an artifact carrying the fix is contrary evidence (D) and blocks as before.

**(H) WHAT THIS ANCHOR DOES NOT CHANGE.** (1) §7.1, §11.4.1, §11.4.6: no false PASS or FAIL claim, and no "verified", "fixed" or "passing" without captured machine evidence; PROVISIONAL-DONE claims "implemented and wired, verification scheduled" and nothing more. (2) The evidence required for VERIFIED-DONE (§11.4.5, §11.4.69, §11.4.107, §11.4.108 four layers with the runtime signature on a clean target, §11.4.123 rock-solid proof, §11.4.226 class floor, anti-echo rule and fix-kind honesty) is unchanged in full: this anchor adds a labelled state before it and lowers nothing in it. (3) The Status closed set and the §11.4.33 type-aware closure vocabulary. (4) §9 / §9.1 / §9.3 absolute data safety (hardlinked backup before any destructive operation, rollback never forward) and §12 / §12.6 host-session safety (verification jobs stay inside the 60 % memory ceiling and never suspend or sign the user out): optimism never licenses skipping either, and a background job that would breach them does not run. (5) §11.4.197 / §11.4.227(A): "done" still means implemented AND wired; a present-but-unreferenced change is not PROVISIONAL-DONE. (6) Every blocking gate and seam keeps blocking on a FAIL. (7) The Operator-blocked and Obsolete lifecycles. (8) The Markdown-only documentation rule: format exports stay off by default and this anchor adds none.

**(I) HOW THIS REFINES THE EXISTING DONE AND CLOSURE ANCHORS (exact).** §11.4.15: its `Ready for testing` and `In testing` rows (a fix has landed and waits for, or is in, a deploy cycle) are the Status carriers of PROVISIONAL-DONE; this anchor adds the `Verification:` line, the pre-scheduled job, the done-for-planning counting and the bound, and leaves `Reopened` and the closure value untouched. §11.4.33: the type-aware closure values mean VERIFIED-DONE and only that. §11.4.34 / §11.4.55: the automatic reopen uses their attribution, closed reason vocabulary and counter, with no new reopen vocabulary. §11.4.7: contrary evidence (D) IS its same-conditions positive evidence for a demotion. §11.4.108: its "DONE" (clause 1, the runtime signature verified on a clean deployment) is VERIFIED-DONE, and clause 2's per-fix runtime-signature registry is the content of the scheduled job. §11.4.123: "fixed" and "completed" mean VERIFIED-DONE; the 2026-06-03 mandate is not superseded and the two operator texts are reconciled by the label (the tension paragraph above). §11.4.226: seam (1)'s class floor binds the VERIFIED-DONE write, seam (4)'s per-status precondition binds this state (clause C), seam (5)'s fix-kind honesty binds it, and seam (6)'s freshness queue drains the provisional set first. §11.4.197: the provisional state is bounded. §11.4.135: on promotion the job's check becomes the permanent regression guard. §11.4.115: RED on the pre-fix artifact stays expected and never contrary. Each of §11.4.15, §11.4.33, §11.4.108, §11.4.123 and §11.4.226 carries a dated refinement note pointing here.

**Honest boundary (§11.4.6).** The gate shipped with this anchor proves the RULE is present exactly once, complete, and cross-referenced in both directions across the canon, the four context mirrors, the index, the progressive catalog, the workable-item lifecycle skill and the propagation data pack. It does NOT prove that a consuming project's tracker carries the `Verification:` line, that a job is scheduled at marking, or that the automatic reopen executes. That item-level seam is each consuming project's own tracked work (§11.4.197; by §11.4.227(A) a rule's done state is its seam landing, not its text landing) and is not claimed shipped here. The closed Status set is the tracker's own enforced vocabulary, which is why the state rides on a `Verification:` line instead of a new Status literal.
